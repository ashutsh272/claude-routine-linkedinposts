**The AGI era may have just quietly begun.**

On September 3, OpenAI launched GPT-6 Astra — its most capable model yet — and the benchmark numbers are hard to ignore: 99.9% on ARC-AGI-3, 98% on FrontierMath Tier 4, and a perfect 100% on ExploitBench. These aren't incremental gains; they're tests designed to measure human-level and beyond-human reasoning.

What makes Astra different isn't just raw scores. It can use your computer like a human would — navigating interfaces, running code, and completing multi-step tasks autonomously end-to-end. It was trained on more than 100,000 GPUs at OpenAI's Stargate facility in Texas — by far their largest training run ever.

For professionals in software engineering, cybersecurity, data science, and research, this is a step-change. Agents that can reason, code, and act at this level will reshape workflows across every industry.
