**What if your rough sketch could become production-ready creative in seconds?**

OpenAI shipped ChatGPT Images 2.5 on September 8, and the practical upgrades are worth paying attention to. The standout new feature: Sketch — draw a rough shape or layout, and the model fills it in. Templates for common formats like posters and merchandise are now built-in. Generation latency is down 50% compared to Images 2.0.

For developers and product teams, there are two new API models: GPT-Image-2.5 Flare (optimized for speed and high-volume pipelines) and GPT-Image-2.5 Sunburst (optimized for editing precision and reference-image fidelity). Both are available via the OpenAI API, AWS, and Google Cloud.

The biggest workflow gain: multi-turn editing. You can now point to a specific area of an image with a comment and request a targeted local edit — a meaningful improvement for marketing and design teams iterating on content at scale.
