**Anthropic just made its most powerful model 75% cheaper to run.**

Fable 5.1 launched September 1, and the headline number is striking: cache read costs drop to $0.25 per million tokens — 75% less than Fable 5. For teams running complex coding agents or high-volume document processing, overall costs could fall by as much as 45%.

But the upgrade isn't just about price. Fable 5.1 is meaningfully better at:
→ Long-horizon coding tasks — multi-file projects, full-codebase reviews
→ Scientific work — experiment design, simulation analysis, dense technical diagrams
→ Agentic workflows that require sustained reasoning across large contexts

It ships at the same list price ($10/$50 per million input/output tokens) and is available today on the Claude API, AWS Bedrock, Google Cloud, and Azure.

Anthropic also unveiled Claude Mythos 5.1 — a companion model with specialized safeguards for cybersecurity and life sciences, available through trusted access programs.

For teams already building on Claude, the math just got better. For those still evaluating, the gap to switch is narrowing.
