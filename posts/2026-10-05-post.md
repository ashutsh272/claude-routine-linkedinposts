The enterprise AI talent crisis just got a $100 million solution.

Anthropic just launched the Claude Frontier Academy — a bold program aimed at training 10,000 Frontier Deployed Engineers by end of 2027. Think of it as a medical residency for AI: a 12-week hands-on program paired with in-person training, designed to close the widening gap between AI capabilities and enterprise deployment skills.

Major organizations are already onboard — Accenture, Bain, McKinsey, Morgan Stanley, Deloitte, Capgemini, Commonwealth Bank of Australia, and Novo Nordisk — sending their engineers through the program. Successful graduates earn a recognized Claude Frontier Deployed Engineer badge.

This isn't just about upskilling. It's about standardizing what "AI-ready" means at scale. As models become more capable, the bottleneck shifts from the AI itself to the humans deploying it effectively.
