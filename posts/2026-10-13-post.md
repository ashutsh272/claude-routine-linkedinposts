AI is now being deployed to defend the systems we all depend on.

Anthropic just launched the Anthropic Cyber Mission — a long-term initiative to help security defenders protect critical infrastructure and open-source software using AI.

Two concrete programs launched immediately:

**Critical Infrastructure Defense Program** — Trusted security providers get direct access to Claude models, Anthropic engineers on-site, and threat research to protect operational technology (think power grids, water systems, and industrial control systems).

**OSS Scanner** — A free, opt-in service that gives open-source projects continuous security scans using Anthropic's most capable models.

This is a meaningful shift: AI companies are no longer just building offense-capable tools and hoping for the best. Anthropic is making an explicit bet that AI can — and should — tilt the balance toward defenders.

For security teams, software maintainers, and infrastructure operators, this is worth paying attention to.
