1,000 AI agents just proved Fermat's Last Theorem in 30 hours. That's not a headline from 2030 — it happened last week with Anthropic's newly released Claude Fable 5.2.

Anthropic released Claude Fable 5.2 and Claude Mythos 5.2 on November 9, the same underlying model with different access tiers. Here's what stands out:

- **30% faster** than the previous generation (Fable/Mythos 5.1)
- Solved two **open problems in black hole physics** using holographic duality
- Modeled 28 of 34 **viral capsid structures** for biotech research
- One engineer used 1,000+ subagents to build a **prototype OS that boots on a laptop** — in 8 days
- Over **90% of changes** to Anthropic's own codebase are now written by Claude

Fable 5.2 is available to all users today on AWS, Google Cloud, and Azure (API ID: `claude-fable-5-2`). Mythos 5.2 is restricted to vetted cybersecurity and life sciences organizations.
