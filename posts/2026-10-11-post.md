AI just got 75% cheaper — without sacrificing capability.

Anthropic released Claude Haiku 5.5 this week, and the numbers are striking. Input costs dropped from $1.00 to $0.10 per million tokens. On agentic computer-use benchmarks, accuracy jumped from 15.7% to 72.4%. Terminal coding tasks went from 0% to 39%.

For enterprises, the real impact is on scale economics. High-volume workflows — customer support, document processing, CRM updates, financial summaries — can now run at a fraction of previous cost. Haiku 5.5 also introduces an adjustable "effort" setting, letting you trade cost for intelligence depending on task complexity.

The model is available on AWS, Google Cloud, and Azure, so it fits existing infrastructure without friction.

The takeaway: faster, cheaper, smarter small models are closing the gap with what "frontier" meant just six months ago. That changes the calculus for any team deciding how much AI to embed in their operations.
