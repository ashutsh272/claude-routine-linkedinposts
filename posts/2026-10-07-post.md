The AI talent gap just got a $100 million answer.

Anthropic announced the Claude Frontier Academy — a program committed to training 10,000 Frontier Deployed Engineers (FDEs) by end of 2027. Think of it like a medical residency for AI: multi-day in-person sessions with Anthropic engineers, simulated enterprise deployments, graded practicals, and 12-week on-the-job residencies.

First cohorts are already running with engineers from Accenture, Bain, Deloitte, McKinsey, Morgan Stanley, and Novo Nordisk — in San Francisco, New York, and London.

Why does this matter? The #1 bottleneck for enterprise AI adoption isn't the technology — it's the people who can deploy it safely at scale. Anthropic is building the certification infrastructure that separates AI-curious engineers from production-ready AI engineers.

If you're in enterprise tech, the Claude Frontier Deployed Engineer badge may become one of the most sought-after credentials in your industry.
