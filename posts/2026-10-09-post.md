Most AI companies are racing to build offense. Anthropic just bet on defense.

Yesterday, Anthropic announced the **Anthropic Cyber Mission** — a long-term initiative focused on giving defenders the tools, research, and resources they need to secure software and critical systems. This isn't a small blog post; it's backed by two concrete launches:

1. **Critical Infrastructure Defense Program** — a partnership with firms like Accenture and Dragos aimed at protecting operational technology and government systems.
2. **OSS Scanner** — a free, opt-in service that scans open-source projects for vulnerabilities before they become the next supply chain incident.

Why does this matter to you? Because cybersecurity has long been an asymmetric game — attackers move fast, defenders react. AI can flip that equation, but only if AI companies actively invest in defensive tooling rather than leaving it as an afterthought.

Anthropic is making a public commitment that Claude's capabilities should make systems *safer*, not just smarter.
