**An AI just solved 10 math problems that stumped the world's best researchers — for $2,000.**

OpenAI revealed this week that an internal version of "Astra" — their next major model family — resolved ten long-standing open problems in mathematics and theoretical computer science. The problems span group theory, high-dimensional sphere packing, quantum complexity, lattice cryptography, and extremal combinatorics — areas where top researchers have been stuck for decades.

The total compute cost: roughly $2,000 at current API rates.

OpenAI published a 249-page manuscript alongside machine-verifiable Lean 4 proofs for all ten results on GitHub. Fields Medal winner Timothy Gowers said he would recommend one of the proofs for publication in a top journal without hesitation.

This is not just a benchmark. It's a signal that AI is becoming a genuine collaborator in formal reasoning and scientific discovery — not just a faster search engine or code autocomplete.

For professionals in R&D, engineering, cryptography, or any field built on mathematical foundations: the pace of AI-assisted discovery just shifted.
