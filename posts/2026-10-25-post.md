What if the specialized knowledge your AI agent needs to handle your company's workflows could be packaged, shared, and run on any platform?

That's now reality. Anthropic just donated Agent Skills — its open standard for giving AI agents specialized procedural knowledge — to the Agentic AI Foundation (AAIF) under the Linux Foundation.

In less than a year since launch, the adoption numbers are striking: 70+ agent platforms including GitHub Copilot, ChatGPT, and Cursor have already integrated the standard. Nearly 280 million skills have been installed, with over 1 million published.

Agent Skills builds on the same open-standard philosophy as MCP (Model Context Protocol), which Anthropic also donated to the AAIF. Together, they're laying the foundation for truly interoperable, composable AI agents that work across tools and platforms — not just within one vendor's ecosystem.

For developers and businesses, the practical implication is clear: workflows you build once can run on any agent platform. Less vendor lock-in. More portability.
