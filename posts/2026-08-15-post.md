**Google just cut AI coding costs in half — and the performance numbers are even more impressive than the price.**

On August 13, Google launched Gemini 3.7 Flash, its most capable "workhorse" model yet for software engineering and agentic workflows.

The gains are real and measurable. On DeepSWE v1.1 — a benchmark testing autonomous issue resolution on real codebases — Gemini 3.7 Flash jumped from 49.0% to 65.3%. On FrontierCode 1.1, it climbed from 34.4% to 43.6%. Google says the model outperformed comparable offerings from Anthropic and OpenAI across nine key benchmarks.

For engineers, this means fewer failed agent loops, better first-pass code accuracy, and a model that clarifies ambiguous intent rather than guessing wrong. Knowledge workers in finance, law, and biosciences also get meaningfully improved reasoning and domain accuracy.

The pricing makes it hard to ignore: $0.75 per million input tokens through December 2026 — half the launch cost of Gemini 3.6 Flash.

Gemini 3.7 Flash is live now in Google AI Studio, Google Antigravity, and Android Studio, and it already powers Gemini Spark for AI Pro and Ultra subscribers across 160+ countries.
