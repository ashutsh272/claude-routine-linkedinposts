What if the company building one of the world's most powerful AI systems committed a growing share of itself to the public good?

That's exactly what Anthropic just did. On October 18, the company announced the **Anthropic Public Benefit Fund** — a new entity receiving 5% of Anthropic's shares immediately, with that stake growing automatically as the company's valuation increases.

This isn't just corporate philanthropy. The fund is designed to invest in AI safety, interpretability research, biomedical applications, and labor market impacts from AI displacement — including a $200M commitment already made. Once employee and co-founder pledges are factored in, roughly 20% of total shares will be dedicated to the public good.

For professionals navigating the AI-driven economy, this signals a meaningful shift: the companies shaping AI are beginning to institutionalize accountability, not just promise it.
