**Three AI model tiers. One launch. And a new definition of what AI can do at work.**

On July 9, OpenAI released GPT-5.6 to the public — not one model, but a full family built to fit every workload:

- **Sol** (flagship): The most capable model OpenAI has shipped. Scores 53.6 on Agents' Last Exam — 13 points ahead of the nearest competitor — and is 54% more token-efficient for AI coding tasks. Built for complex reasoning, long-horizon agentic work, and security-sensitive tasks. ($5 / $30 per 1M tokens)
- **Terra** (balanced): Matches GPT-5.5 performance at 2× lower cost. ($2.50 / $15 per 1M tokens)
- **Luna** (fast): Highest throughput, lowest price — designed for high-volume pipelines. ($1 / $6 per 1M tokens)

Alongside the models, OpenAI launched **ChatGPT Work** — an agent built to carry out entire jobs autonomously, not just answer individual questions.

The framing matters: the number (5.6) marks the generation; Sol, Terra, and Luna are durable tiers that will each advance on their own cadence. This isn't a one-time release — it's a new product architecture.

The shift from AI assistant to AI worker is here. The real question is which tier your workflows belong in.
