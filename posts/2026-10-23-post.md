280 million installs. 70+ platforms. Now it belongs to everyone.

Anthropic just donated Agent Skills — its standard for giving AI agents task-specific knowledge and workflows — to the Agentic AI Foundation (AAIF) under the Linux Foundation.

Here's why this matters for professionals building with AI:

Agent Skills started inside Anthropic's Claude Code team to solve a real problem: general AI models are great at broad reasoning, but they lack the procedural know-how that specific business workflows require. A "skill" is simply a folder with a SKILL.md file describing a task or domain, plus any scripts or reference material the agent needs.

Since the standard launched in December 2025, adoption has been staggering:
- 70+ platforms integrated it, including ChatGPT, Cursor, GitHub Copilot, and VS Code
- ~1 million skills published
- ~280 million skills installed

By moving governance to the Linux Foundation — alongside the Model Context Protocol (MCP) — Anthropic is betting on open, vendor-neutral standards as the long-term foundation for agentic AI. Engineers from Anthropic, Google, OpenAI, and others will maintain the spec as individuals, not corporate representatives.

This is how durable developer ecosystems get built.
