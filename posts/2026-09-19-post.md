**What if your household had a shared AI that everyone could actually trust?**

Google Labs just expanded CC — its experimental AI agent — to work for up to six family members at once, with each person choosing exactly what it can see.

CC now has its own Google account and coordinates across everyone's calendars, emails, and tasks. Each morning it generates a shared brief called "Your Day Ahead" — syncing school runs, bill payments, sports practices, and dinner plans for the whole household.

What makes this different from a shared calendar? CC actively *manages* the coordination. It runs on Google's Antigravity agent harness powered by Gemini, meaning it can take actions — not just display information.

As AI agents move from individual productivity tools to shared household infrastructure, the key design question becomes: how do you build trust across multiple people with different privacy preferences? Google's answer is granular, per-person data sharing controls.

Still a Google Labs experiment, but this is a concrete preview of what domestic AI actually looks like in practice.
