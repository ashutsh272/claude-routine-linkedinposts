AI just got a "find it and fix it" superpower for software security — and every development and security team should be paying attention.

OpenAI this week expanded **GPT-5.5-Cyber**, a model purpose-built to go beyond finding vulnerabilities to automatically generating, testing, and delivering verified patches. Integrated with Codex Security, it can trace attack paths across large codebases, confirm whether flaws are actually exploitable, and produce full remediation evidence — all in a single automated workflow.

OpenAI is also opening access to 30+ cybersecurity vendors and co-funding **"Patch the Planet"** — a partnership with Trail of Bits and HackerOne aimed at helping open-source maintainers close vulnerabilities at scale.

Why it matters: security backlogs cost organizations billions annually. Compressing the "find → triage → patch → verify" cycle from weeks to minutes isn't a research demo anymore — it's heading into the products your teams already use.

The real shift here isn't just speed. It's accountability: a model that delivers a tested, verified fix changes who owns the remediation decision — and that's a meaningful change for AppSec, engineering, and compliance alike.
