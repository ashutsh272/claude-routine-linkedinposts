Stop repeating yourself to your AI assistant.

Anthropic just fixed one of the most common friction points in AI workflows. As of August 25, Claude's memory is now unified across both Chat and Cowork — meaning context you share in a conversation automatically carries over into agentic tasks, and vice versa.

Before this update, Chat and Cowork kept entirely separate memories. Switching between them meant re-briefing Claude from scratch on your role, preferences, and project context. Not anymore.

What changed:
- Memory updates in real time as you work — not in a delayed summary after the session ends
- You can review, edit, or delete any stored memory item by topic in Settings > Memory
- Sensitive topics (health, beliefs) are off by default — opt in only if you want them remembered
- Available on Free, Pro, and Max plans across web, desktop, and mobile

For professionals bouncing between quick chat queries and longer automated Cowork tasks, this is the kind of quality-of-life improvement that compounds over time. The AI finally feels like it knows you.
