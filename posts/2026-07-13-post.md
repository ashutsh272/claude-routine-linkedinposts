**For the first time ever, Meta is charging for its AI — and the model it's betting on just set a new bar for agentic work.**

On July 9, Meta Superintelligence Labs launched Muse Spark 1.1, the company's first model available through a paid API. After years of releasing open-source models for free, Meta has entered the enterprise AI market with a monetized offering.

What makes this relevant to professionals:

→ **1M-token context window** with active compaction — designed for long-horizon tasks that need to track and retrieve earlier work across thousands of steps
→ **Native computer use** — the model can operate software across multiple applications and adapt to real-time information changes mid-task
→ **Multi-agent orchestration** built in — purpose-built for workflows that unfold across tools, not one-shot queries

Pricing: $1.25 / $4.25 per million input/output tokens. New developers receive $20 in free credits.

Meta now believes it has a model worth paying for. That alone signals something has changed.
