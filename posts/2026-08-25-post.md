What if the AI model running in your pocket was also analyzing satellite imagery in orbit?

Google's Gemma open models just crossed 1 billion cumulative downloads — a milestone that puts the real scale of open-source AI adoption in sharp relief.

Here's what's remarkable: developers haven't just downloaded Gemma, they've remixed it. Over 100,000 distinct Gemma variants have been published — fine-tuned for specific languages, tasks, and hardware targets. The "Gemmaverse" now spans everything from general assistants to highly specialized research tools.

The real-world deployments are striking:
→ NASA, Satlyt, and Starcloud are running Gemma directly in orbit for onboard satellite image analysis and intersatellite communications
→ India's Aarogya Setu 2.0 (100M+ users) integrated Gemma 4 into its national health app
→ 1,600+ projects were submitted to the Gemma Challenge on Kaggle

This is what the open-source AI moment actually looks like — not just downloads, but deployment at global scale across space, healthcare, and beyond.
