The era of CPUs built specifically for AI agents just went from announcement to reality.

NVIDIA this week hand-delivered its first Vera CPU systems to Anthropic, OpenAI, SpaceXAI, and Oracle Cloud Infrastructure — marking the moment agentic AI infrastructure moves into production at the world's top labs.

What makes Vera different? It's the first processor designed from the ground up for how AI agents actually work: orchestrating tools, executing code, processing data, and running simulations *between* model calls — keeping GPUs fully fed rather than sitting idle.

The specs back up the claim: 88 custom Olympus cores, 1.2 TB/s memory bandwidth, and up to 1.8x faster task completion compared with traditional x86 CPUs on agentic workloads.

For professionals building or deploying AI agents, this matters. Faster orchestration means agents can act sooner, iterate tighter, and cost less per task. The CPU — long a commodity afterthought in AI stacks — is now a first-class performance lever.
