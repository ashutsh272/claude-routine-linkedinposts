Only 20 companies are allowed to use OpenAI's most powerful model — and the US government has a say in who they are.

OpenAI just unveiled **GPT-5.6**, a new three-model family: **Sol** (flagship), **Terra** (balanced, 2x cheaper than GPT-5.5), and **Luna** (fast and affordable). Sol is currently the most capable model OpenAI has shipped — setting a new state-of-the-art on Terminal-Bench 2.1 for complex, multi-step coding workflows.

Two standout features: a new **max reasoning effort** mode that gives Sol more time to think through hard problems, and **ultra mode** — a multi-agent setup where Sol orchestrates subagents to tackle work in parallel. This is OpenAI's clearest signal yet that agentic, multi-step AI is the near-term direction for serious work.

Terra and Luna bring these advances down the cost curve, which matters for developers building at scale.

General availability is expected within weeks.
