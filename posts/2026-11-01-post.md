The AI safety company that built Claude just filed to go public — and it could reshape how the entire industry is funded.

Anthropic has filed an S-1 registration statement with the SEC for a proposed IPO of its Class A common stock, targeting a listing on the Nasdaq Global Select Market under the ticker "ANT." Goldman Sachs and Morgan Stanley are leading the offering.

What makes this remarkable: Anthropic is structured as a Public Benefit Corporation, with a stated mission to "ensure the world safely makes the transition through transformative AI." This is not a typical Silicon Valley growth-at-all-costs story — it's a bet that safety-first AI can be both a public good and a commercially viable enterprise.

For professionals in tech, finance, and enterprise software, this IPO will set a precedent. It signals that AI safety is no longer a niche concern — it's a business model. Expect this listing to draw intense scrutiny from regulators, investors, and competitors alike.

The prospectus is not yet effective, so no shares can be purchased yet.
