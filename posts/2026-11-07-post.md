What does it look like when AI moves from pilot project to government infrastructure?

Anthropic just expanded its deal with the U.S. General Services Administration — now offering a **40% discount on Claude** to every level of government: federal, state, local, tribal, and territorial. No minimum spend. No lock-in. Pay only for what you use.

The practical impact is significant: Anthropic estimates this will save the government approximately **$1 billion**. Agencies are already deploying Claude for legacy code modernization, records management, backlog reduction, and cybersecurity response — work that has historically consumed enormous budgets with slow results.

For public sector technologists, IT leaders, and anyone selling into government: the procurement friction just dropped meaningfully. Claude for Government is offered at FedRAMP High, and access flows through Amazon Bedrock and Google Cloud Vertex AI — meaning agencies can plug into existing cloud infrastructure without new compliance overhead.

This isn't just a government story. It's a signal that enterprise AI adoption at scale is shifting from "should we?" to "how fast can we implement?"
