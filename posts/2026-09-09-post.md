Europe just made history in AI.

Mistral AI announced yesterday that it has closed a €3 billion Series D — the largest equity fundraising round ever completed by a European technology company. Samsung Electronics led the round, joined by BlackRock, EQT's Scaleup Europe Fund, and even the Grand Duchy of Luxembourg, putting Mistral's valuation north of €21 billion just three years after the company launched.

Mistral now serves 125+ enterprise clients — including Airbus, ASML, and HSBC — across 20 countries. CEO Arthur Mensch says they're on track to cross $1 billion in ARR before year-end.

What sets this apart: Mistral isn't optimizing for hyperscaler dependency. They're using this capital to build and own data centers while advancing "sovereign AI" — frontier models that European governments and enterprises can rely on without routing sensitive workloads through US infrastructure.

For professionals navigating enterprise AI procurement and data residency requirements, a well-capitalized, frontier-capable European alternative changes the competitive and regulatory landscape considerably.
