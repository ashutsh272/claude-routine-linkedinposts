**What happens when 1 billion ChatGPT users includes millions of teenagers?**

OpenAI answered that question yesterday — not just with a policy, but a product.

The company launched **ChatGPT for Teens** on August 18, rolling out globally to users ages 13–17. The experience auto-detects teen accounts using AI-powered age prediction and activates stronger protections: blocking content around self-harm, eating disorders, and explicit material; adding Study Mode with homework reminders and collaborative problem-solving; and introducing "Quiet Hours" and "Study Hours" parents or teens can configure.

Most striking: OpenAI is explicitly designing against emotional dependence — nudging teens to take breaks after 90 minutes of active use and preventing the parasocial dynamics that have raised concerns with younger users.

For product teams, educators, and organizations deploying AI tools, this signals a maturing moment for the industry. Responsible AI is no longer a policy checkbox — it's becoming a UX requirement.
