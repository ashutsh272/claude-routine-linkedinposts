**The AI arms race just entered the public markets.**

Anthropic has officially filed its S-1 with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission, taking the first formal step toward an IPO on the Nasdaq Global Select Market under the ticker symbol "ANT."

This is a defining moment — not just for Anthropic, but for the entire AI industry. Goldman Sachs and Morgan Stanley are leading the offering, signaling serious institutional confidence in AI safety-focused frontier labs as a durable business model.

For professionals tracking AI, this matters for a few reasons:

→ It validates that building safety-first AI is a viable commercial strategy
→ It will force transparency: S-1 filings require detailed disclosure of revenue, risks, and competitive positioning
→ It could accelerate enterprise AI adoption as organizations gain more visibility into Anthropic's roadmap and financial health

The share count and price range haven't been set yet, but the direction of travel is clear.
