**OpenAI's Greg Brockman just called GPT-6 Astra a "generational leap" — and said it may represent the beginning of AGI.**

Released September 3, GPT-6 Astra starts rolling out broadly today. Here's what matters for professionals:

**Computer use**: Astra can navigate your computer autonomously — clicking, typing, running searches — without step-by-step instructions. It operates more like a skilled colleague than a tool.

**1M+ context window**: Feed it an entire codebase, financial archive, or legal dataset in a single session. Legora already reported a 40% performance improvement on financial document workflows.

**Unprecedented capability**: Astra is the first AI model to reach OpenAI's "Critical" cybersecurity threshold — it can autonomously identify unknown vulnerabilities across complex systems.

Available now on ChatGPT Plus, Pro, Business, and Enterprise, plus the OpenAI API and AWS.

This isn't a point release. Every team building on AI should be paying attention.
