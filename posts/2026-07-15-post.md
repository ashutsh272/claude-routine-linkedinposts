**What if every K-12 teacher in America had a premium AI assistant — completely free?**

On July 14, Anthropic launched Claude for Teachers, giving verified US K-12 educators free access to premium Claude features for a full year (sign-ups open through June 30, 2027).

This isn't just a product launch. It's a calculated move in a growing race — Google, OpenAI, and Khan Academy are all competing to shape how AI enters the classroom.

What makes Claude for Teachers stand out:

→ **State-aligned lesson planning** — Claude draws on curricula mapped to standards across all 50 states, scaffolding plans and student-facing materials teachers can revise instantly
→ **Student data analysis** — hand Claude a folder of diagnostics, attendance, and notes; it builds a picture of where every student stands
→ **FERPA-compliant by design** — co-developed with the American Federation of Teachers; teacher data is never used for model training
→ **Claude Code and Cowork included** — automating workflows, not just generating suggestions

The generation learning to use AI in school becomes the workforce using it everywhere else. That's the long game.
