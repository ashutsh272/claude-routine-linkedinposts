**One of the largest tech IPOs in history just closed — and it's an AI safety company.**

Anthropic officially closed its initial public offering on November 20, 2026, selling 1.127 billion Class A shares at $83 per share on the Nasdaq Global Select Market (ticker: ANT). The IPO alone generated roughly $91.6 billion in net proceeds for the company.

But here's what makes this story unique: alongside the public offering, Anthropic simultaneously closed a $26 billion private placement with a who's-who of the tech world — AMD, Broadcom, Google, Micron, Microsoft, NVIDIA, and TSMC. Total combined proceeds: ~$119.5 billion.

For professionals watching the AI landscape, this matters for three reasons:

1. It signals institutional confidence that safety-focused AI development is commercially viable at scale.
2. The strategic investor list (essentially the entire chip and cloud ecosystem) means Anthropic now has deep infrastructure partnerships baked in.
3. It sets a valuation benchmark that will ripple through every AI startup fundraise for years.

The race to build transformative AI is now publicly priced.
