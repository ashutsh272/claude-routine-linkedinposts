What if the most consequential AI safety company of our era just became publicly accountable to shareholders?

Anthropic closed its IPO on November 20, 2026, raising approximately $93.5 billion from public investors — with shares priced at $83 each and trading on Nasdaq under the ticker "ANT." Alongside the IPO, a concurrent private placement raised an additional ~$26 billion from seven strategic investors: AMD, Broadcom, Google, Micron, Microsoft, NVIDIA, and TSMC. Total combined gross proceeds: nearly $119.5 billion.

This is one of the largest tech IPOs in history — and it's for an AI company built around safety.

For professionals, this moment matters. It signals that AI safety isn't a niche concern; it's a trillion-dollar bet the market is willing to make. It also raises a real question: how will public market pressures shape decisions at a company that has consistently chosen caution over speed?
