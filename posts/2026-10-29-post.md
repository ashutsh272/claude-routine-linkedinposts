What if AI could compress decades of scientific research into years?

Anthropic just took a significant step toward that future. Yesterday, the company expanded its AI for Science program to include 20 major scientific foundations, institutions, and research projects — including the NSF, HHMI Janelia Research Campus, Wellcome Sanger Institute, Allen Institute, and the Simons Foundation.

The program provides free Claude credits to researchers working on high-impact science. Since launching in May 2025, it has supported hundreds of projects across biology, medicine, physics, chemistry, climate science, and mathematics.

One standout development: Anthropic and HHMI Janelia co-created the Model Hardware Standard (MHS) — a shared specification that lets AI agents safely operate real lab instruments. This could fundamentally change how research labs work.

For professionals in life sciences, research institutions, or organizations bridging AI and science — this is worth watching closely.
