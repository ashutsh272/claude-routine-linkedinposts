Anthropic just released what may be their most significant models to date — and the benchmark numbers are hard to ignore.

Claude Fable 5.2 and Claude Mythos 5.2 launched November 9th, built on the same underlying architecture but tuned for different audiences. Fable 5.2 is available to everyone and delivers real jumps in coding and reasoning: 69.9% on SWE v2, 74.2% on Humanity's Last Exam with tools, and 89.3% on OSWorld 2.1. Mythos 5.2, available only to vetted cybersecurity and life sciences organizations, scored 89.6% on CVE Rediscovery.

What stands out beyond the benchmarks: output is 30% faster than the previous generation, safety guardrails now block 55% fewer legitimate requests, and anti-distillation protections reportedly cut harvested data by 90-99%.

For developers and enterprises building on AI infrastructure, this release signals that frontier models are simultaneously getting more capable, faster, and safer — without the usual trade-offs.
