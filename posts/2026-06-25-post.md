The most powerful AI model class is now in your hands.

Anthropic just launched **Claude Fable 5** — the first time a Mythos-class model has been made generally available. This isn't an incremental update. Fable 5 is state-of-the-art on nearly all tested benchmarks, with exceptional performance across software engineering, scientific research, knowledge work, and vision tasks.

For professionals, this changes the calculus. Capabilities that were previously limited to a narrow group of researchers and cyberdefenders — via Claude Mythos — are now accessible through the standard Claude interface. Anthropic simultaneously released **Claude Mythos 5** for cyberdefenders and critical infrastructure providers through Project Glasswing, in collaboration with the US government.

The safety architecture is worth noting: queries on certain sensitive topics route to Claude Opus 4.8 instead, with safeguards designed to trigger in under 5% of sessions on average. This is a meaningful attempt to balance frontier capability with responsible deployment.

Bottom line: the frontier just moved — and this time, it's open to all of us.
