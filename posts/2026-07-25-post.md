**Frontier AI just got 50% cheaper.**

Anthropic launched Claude Opus 5 yesterday, and the headline is hard to ignore: near-Fable 5 performance at exactly half the cost — $5 per million input tokens vs. $10 for Fable 5.

For teams deploying AI at scale, this is more than an incremental upgrade. Opus 5 outscores its predecessor (Opus 4.8) on 8 of 13 benchmarks, including agentic coding, computer use, long-horizon knowledge work, and multidisciplinary reasoning. It now tops the charts on Frontier-Bench and GDPval-AA.

What makes it immediately practical is a new **effort toggle** — low, medium, or high — letting you dial in cost vs. capability per task. Routine queries run cheap; complex analysis gets full power.

Opus 5 is already the default model on Claude Max and the strongest model on Claude Pro. API pricing stays at $5/$25 per million tokens (input/output) — the same as Opus 4.8 — meaning you get meaningfully better performance at the same rate.

If you're evaluating LLMs for enterprise workflows, this changes the cost-performance math.
