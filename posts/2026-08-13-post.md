**What if the best frontier AI model cost half as much as the competition?**

xAI just released Grok 4.6 — and the pricing is turning heads.

At $2 per million input tokens and $6 per million output tokens, Grok 4.6 matches GPT-5.6 Sol and Claude Fable 5 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index while costing roughly half the price of rival frontier models. It also claimed the #1 spot on the Databricks leaderboard with a 1753 ELO score on Chatbot Arena.

But it's not just about benchmarks. Grok 4.6 is built explicitly for long-running agents — the kind of multi-step, autonomous work that's increasingly where enterprise AI value lives. It's available day one in Cursor, Grok Build (now with native subagent views and Plan Mode), OpenRouter, Vercel, and Cloudflare.

For teams evaluating AI infrastructure costs, this release is worth a hard look. Frontier performance at half the price isn't a minor footnote — it's the kind of disruption that reshapes procurement decisions.
