**AI agents can now build the simulation worlds where the next generation of robots learns to think.**

At its SIGGRAPH 2026 keynote yesterday, NVIDIA expanded its Agent Toolkit with new Omniverse libraries — enabling AI agents to autonomously prepare 3D content for physical AI simulation. Jensen Huang put it plainly: "The physical AI era will be built in simulation first."

Until now, preparing simulation-ready 3D environments for robotics and autonomous vehicle training was slow, manual, expert-only work. These new libraries change that:

→ **RTX sensor simulation** — agents can generate photorealistic sensor data (lidar, radar, cameras) from existing 3D scenes
→ **GPU-accelerated physics** — real-world physics validated at scale, in agent-driven workflows
→ **Asset validation** — agents inspect and flag simulation-readiness issues automatically
→ **SimReady Blender** — a sample workflow integrating Omniverse + NemoClaw directly into Blender

All libraries are open-source on GitHub. SideFX (Houdini) and PTC are already integrating them. On DGX Station, the full stack — NemoClaw, Nemotron 3 Ultra, Omniverse tools, and a secure runtime — runs locally.

For teams building physical AI systems, the bottleneck of manually curating simulation data just got a lot smaller.
