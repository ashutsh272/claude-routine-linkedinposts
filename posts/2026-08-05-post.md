**100,000 researchers. Free frontier AI access. $250 million committed through 2027.**

OpenAI just launched "ChatGPT for Academic Researchers" — a program giving scientists, mathematicians, and engineers at select institutions free access to the GPT-5.6 model family, including Sol, its most capable scientific reasoning tier.

Starting with 10,000 researchers this summer at institutions like the Institute for Advanced Study and École normale supérieure, the rollout expands through 2027. Each participant gets:

→ Access to ChatGPT, ChatGPT Work, and Codex
→ Higher usage limits and expanded deep research tools
→ Business-grade privacy — data is not used for model training by default
→ Up to 4 collaborators per workspace

Two days ago, OpenAI's Astra solved 10 open math problems for $2,000 in compute. Now OpenAI is placing frontier models directly in the hands of the people doing the research.

The acceleration of AI-assisted scientific discovery is no longer a future scenario — it's an active program with a budget.
