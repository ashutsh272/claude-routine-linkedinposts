**What if an AI could run your full bioinformatics pipeline, generate a reproducible figure, and trace every step — in a single conversation?**

Anthropic just made that real. On June 30, they launched **Claude Science** — a dedicated AI workbench built for researchers, not as a chatbot, but as a complete working environment.

What sets it apart:

→ 60+ pre-configured tools for genomics, single-cell analysis, proteomics, structural biology, and cheminformatics
→ Native rendering of 3D protein structures, genome browser tracks, and chemical drawings
→ Every output ships with the exact code, environment, and full conversation that produced it — reproducible months later, by anyone on your team
→ Deep integration with PubMed, Jupyter, and R — no tool-switching required

Claude Science is in beta for Pro, Max, Team, and Enterprise subscribers. Anthropic is also funding up to 50 research projects with $30,000 in credits through July 15.

For pharma, biotech, and academic researchers, this isn't a productivity tweak. It's a new kind of infrastructure for how science gets done.
