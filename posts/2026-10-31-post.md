What if AI could accelerate scientific discovery by orders of magnitude? That's exactly what Anthropic is betting on.

Anthropic just expanded its **AI for Science** program — and the scale is remarkable.

Twenty of the world's leading scientific foundations, institutions, and research projects now have access to free Claude credits for high-impact work. Partners include the US National Science Foundation, the Wellcome Sanger Institute, the Francis Crick Institute, EMBL, and the Broad Institute's gnomAD project (which pools genomic data from over 800,000 people).

What makes this notable: Anthropic isn't just giving individual researchers access — they're going upstream to the funders and institutions that shape entire research agendas. Projects are expected to produce open datasets, open-source tools, and shared standards that benefit the whole scientific community.

Since May 2025, the program has supported hundreds of projects across biology, medicine, physics, chemistry, climate science, and mathematics.

For professionals in life sciences, biotech, and research-driven industries: the pace at which AI is entering foundational science is accelerating fast.
