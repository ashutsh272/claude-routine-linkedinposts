The AI safety company is going public.

Anthropic just filed its S-1 with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission, officially kicking off the process for an IPO of its Class A common stock on the Nasdaq Global Select Market under the ticker "ANT."

This is a landmark moment — not just for Anthropic, but for the entire AI industry. Founded in 2021 by former OpenAI researchers, Anthropic has positioned itself as the "safety-first" AI lab, making Claude one of the most widely used enterprise AI assistants. Going public means greater transparency into their financials, burn rate, and the real economics of frontier AI development.

Goldman Sachs and Morgan Stanley are leading the offering. Pricing and share count haven't been set yet.

For professionals building on AI infrastructure, partnering with AI vendors, or simply watching where the industry is heading — this IPO will set a pricing benchmark for what frontier AI is actually worth.
