Singapore is the second most AI-engaged country on Earth, per capita — and Anthropic just made it official.

Anthropic opened its Singapore office yesterday and signed a Memorandum of Understanding with Singapore's Ministry of Digital Development and Information (MDDI). The cooperation covers AI safety evaluation, testing standards, incident response, and responsible AI adoption across public and private sectors.

The numbers behind the announcement are striking. GovTech Singapore already has 1,500+ engineers using Claude Code to modernize government systems. Grab's Claude-powered sales assistant saves 40,000 hours per quarter. DBS, GIC, Sea, and Supabase are all building on Claude in the region.

What makes this notable for professionals isn't just the market signal — it's the model. Anthropic is pairing commercial expansion with formal safety partnerships and in-region infrastructure for regulated industries. That's a template more AI companies will need to follow as governments demand accountability alongside capability.

This is what responsible AI adoption at the national scale looks like.
