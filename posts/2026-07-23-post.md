**Google just made its AI workhorse 17% more efficient — and dropped the price.**

On July 21, Google launched three new Gemini models: **Gemini 3.6 Flash**, **3.5 Flash-Lite**, and the security-focused **3.5 Flash Cyber** — and the numbers are hard to ignore.

3.6 Flash is positioned as Google's everyday workhorse for developers and enterprises running AI at scale. Here's what changed:

→ **17% fewer output tokens** vs. 3.5 Flash (per Artificial Analysis Index)
→ Output pricing cut from $9.00 → **$7.50/M tokens** (input stays at $1.50/M)
→ Coding: 37% → **49% on DeepSWE** benchmark
→ Computer use: 78.4% → **83% on OSWorld-Verified**
→ Knowledge cutoff updated to **March 2026**
→ Now available in **GitHub Copilot**

The 3.5 Flash Cyber variant adds specialized software vulnerability detection — currently restricted to governments and trusted security partners.

Google also teased that **Gemini 4** is on the way.

For teams choosing which model to ship with, token efficiency and pricing often matter as much as raw capability. This update shifts that calculus meaningfully.
