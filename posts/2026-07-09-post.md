**What if you could run enterprise-grade AI agents at 10x lower cost — starting today?**

That's what NVIDIA and LangChain are delivering with **NemoClaw**, a new open reference blueprint for building, evaluating, and deploying enterprise agents — announced yesterday, July 8.

The blueprint combines three layers: **LangChain Deep Agents** (planning, memory, tool use, and long-running task execution), **NVIDIA Nemotron 3 Ultra** (the model backbone), and **NVIDIA OpenShell** (secure, governed deployment). In LangChain's own agent eval suite, Nemotron 3 Ultra scored 0.86 in aggregate — at just $4.48 per task. The next closest model cost $43.48. That's nearly a 10x reduction with no performance tradeoff.

For teams building production AI agents in finance, healthcare, legal, or any regulated industry, the governance story matters as much as the cost. Open stack. Customizable. Deployable on-prem.

EY is already building an implementation practice around it. Baseten, Fireworks, Nebius, and Together AI are providing model serving infrastructure.
