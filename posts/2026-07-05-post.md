**6,000 AI engineers. $2.5 billion. Embedded inside your company.**

That's Microsoft's newest bet on enterprise AI — and it fundamentally changes the deployment model.

On July 2, Microsoft unveiled **Microsoft Frontier Company**, a new operating business that embeds thousands of AI engineers and industry experts directly inside enterprise customers to co-design, deploy, and continuously improve AI systems based on measurable business outcomes.

This isn't a software license. It's large-scale implementation — with Microsoft's full infrastructure and models behind it.

Early partners include the **London Stock Exchange Group, Unilever, and Land O'Lakes**. At $2.5 billion, the commitment is 2.5× larger than Amazon's recently announced forward-deployed engineering program.

The signal is unmistakable: the gap between "buying AI tools" and "getting AI to work" is now so large that Microsoft is willing to put 6,000 people on the problem — on-site.

For enterprise leaders navigating AI adoption, this is worth watching closely.
