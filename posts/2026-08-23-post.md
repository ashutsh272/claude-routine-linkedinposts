**What if the world's most capable cyber-aware AI model could scan your codebase — without ever being steerable toward attacks?**

Anthropic just made that real. Claude Mythos 5 — its frontier model previously limited to vetted security researchers — is now powering vulnerability scanning inside Claude Security for Enterprise customers.

Here's the key design choice: users never interact with the model directly. They get structured outputs: a patch for a vulnerability, a CWE-classified alert, a severity rating. That guardrail is precisely what allows Anthropic to widen access. The model that finds the hole can't be redirected to weaponize it.

What's in the announcement:
• Claude Security (public beta) scans repos with Mythos 5 and returns findings with remediation suggestions
• Plans to integrate Mythos 5 into security partner products
• $35M in credits for a new open-source cyber defense fund
• Expansion of Anthropic's Cyber Verification Program

For security and engineering teams: enterprise-grade AI vulnerability scanning just got a significant capability upgrade.
