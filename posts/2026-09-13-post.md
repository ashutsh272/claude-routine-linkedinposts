**The company that makes the chips just bought the store where the models live.**

NVIDIA announced on September 3 that it will acquire Hugging Face — the world's largest open AI model repository — for $12.93 billion. That's 3 million AI models, 1 million applications, and 18 million developers, now under Jensen Huang's roof.

This is vertical integration at its most complete: NVIDIA already powers the data centers where models are trained. Now it owns the marketplace where those models are shared, discovered, and deployed. For any organization building on AI, that's a meaningful shift in who controls the stack.

There's a reassurance worth noting: Huang says Hugging Face will remain an open platform for the entire ecosystem, and NVIDIA compute will not be required to build or deploy through it. Still — the incentive structures are worth watching.

For AI practitioners and engineering teams, the question is no longer just "which model?" but "who owns the model shelf?"
