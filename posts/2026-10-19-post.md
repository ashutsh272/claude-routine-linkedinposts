What if a leading AI company put roughly 20% of its equity toward the public good — not through donations, but built into its corporate structure?

Anthropic just announced the Anthropic Public Benefit Fund. Starting with 5% of company shares (subject to stockholder approval), the fund's stake can grow by an additional 2% for every 50% increase in Anthropic's valuation above a baseline. Combined with earlier co-founder pledges and an employee equity match program, approximately 20% of Anthropic's equity will ultimately be dedicated to public benefit — with a focus on AI's workforce and economic impacts.

Structured as an LLC rather than a traditional 501(c)(3), the fund gains flexibility to act beyond typical nonprofit constraints. No lobbying, no political giving — just long-term public benefit tied directly to Anthropic's commercial growth.

For leaders in governance, investing, and policy, this offers a compelling new template: aligning commercial incentives with societal accountability from the inside out.
