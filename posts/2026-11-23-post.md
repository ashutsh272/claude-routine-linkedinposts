What does it look like when AI moves from a tool professionals use to one that solves open problems in physics and writes most of a company's production code?

Anthropic released Claude Fable 5.2 and Claude Mythos 5.2 on November 9, 2026 — its most capable models to date. The numbers are striking: 69.9% on FrontierSWE v2 agentic coding (up from 62.3%), outputs up to 30% faster than the previous version, and over 90% of changes now merged into Anthropic's own codebase written by Claude. On the science side, Fable 5.2 solved two open problems in black hole physics and helped design working electronic devices. A coordinated team of 1,000 AI agents formalized Fermat's Last Theorem in roughly 30 hours.

Mythos 5.2 is the restricted variant — aimed at vetted organizations in dual-use fields like life sciences and cybersecurity — and found 10.2% more vulnerabilities than the previous flagship model on internal CVE evaluations.

For professionals building on AI: the gap between "AI assistant" and "AI colleague" is narrowing faster than most roadmaps anticipated.
