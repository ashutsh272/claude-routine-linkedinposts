**What if Google's smartest AI coder now costs half as much?**

That's exactly what happened on August 13 when Google released Gemini 3.7 Flash — its new frontier workhorse model built explicitly for coding and agentic workflows.

The numbers tell the story fast: DeepSWE benchmark jumped from 49% to 65.3%, and FrontierCode improved from 34.4% to 43.6%. That's not a minor iteration — that's a meaningful leap in real-world software engineering tasks, including debugging, issue resolution, and production-ready code generation on the first attempt.

All of this at $0.75 per million input tokens — roughly half the price of its predecessor at launch. The introductory pricing runs through December 31, 2026.

Gemini 3.7 Flash handles a 1M token context window and processes text, images, audio, and video. It's live in Google AI Studio, Android Studio, and the Gemini Enterprise Agent Platform across 160+ countries.

For engineering teams evaluating AI-assisted development, this is a legitimate reason to reassess your current stack.
