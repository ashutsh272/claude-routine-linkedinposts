**What if you could run near-frontier AI agents for a fraction of what you're paying today?**

Anthropic just shipped **Claude Sonnet 5** — and the pricing math is hard to ignore.

Sonnet 5 delivers performance close to Opus 4.8 (Anthropic's previous top model) at an introductory price of **$2 per million input tokens** — roughly 5–7x cheaper than Opus. For developers building agentic workflows, this isn't a minor update. It changes what's economically viable to automate.

What's new under the hood:
- **Autonomous tool use**: Sonnet 5 can independently operate browsers and terminals to complete multi-step tasks without hand-holding
- **Better planning**: improved ability to break down complex goals and execute them reliably
- **Cyber safeguards by default**: real-time detection and blocking of dangerous cybersecurity use cases — on for everyone, out of the box

It's now the **default model for all Claude Free and Pro users**, meaning millions of people upgraded automatically on June 30th.

For teams evaluating where to run their agents: the cost-performance gap just narrowed significantly. The era of "Opus-class reasoning for Sonnet-class prices" is here.
