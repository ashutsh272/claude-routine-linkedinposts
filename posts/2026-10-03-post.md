The biggest bottleneck in enterprise AI isn't the model — it's the engineers who can actually deploy it.

Anthropic just put $100 million behind that thesis. Yesterday they launched Claude Frontier Academy, a program targeting 10,000 newly certified Frontier Deployed Engineers (FDEs) by end of 2027.

The program follows a medical residency model: in-person intensives with Anthropic engineers, simulated enterprise deployment exercises, graded assessments, and a 12-week residency leading real Claude projects inside your own organization. First cohorts are already running in San Francisco, New York, and London — with partners including Accenture, Bain, Deloitte, McKinsey, Morgan Stanley, and Novo Nordisk.

The insight driving this: AI capabilities have outpaced the talent pool that knows how to use them inside real enterprise constraints. As Steve Corfield put it, "A small team of high-agency people with the right skills can transform an entire company" — but finding those people has become the hardest part.

If you're a software engineer with LLM fundamentals, this certification is worth tracking.
