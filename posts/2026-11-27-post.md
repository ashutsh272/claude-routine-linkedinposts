$119.5 billion. That's what the market just said responsible AI is worth.

Anthropic officially closed its IPO on November 20, raising ~$93.5 billion in public markets at $83 per share — with Class A shares now trading on Nasdaq under ticker "ANT." On top of that, a concurrent $26 billion private placement brought in Google, Microsoft, NVIDIA, AMD, Broadcom, Micron, and TSMC as shareholders.

This isn't just a headline number. It's a verdict.

For years, "safety-first AI" was framed as a constraint on commercial ambition. Anthropic's debut rewrites that story. Institutional investors and the world's most powerful semiconductor and cloud companies are betting that the most responsible AI lab is also the most valuable one.

For professionals navigating AI adoption decisions, this changes the calculus: enterprises no longer choose between "capable" and "safe" — they're now one and the same standard.
