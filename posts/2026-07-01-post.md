**What just happened will redefine how governments regulate AI models.**

Today, Claude Fable 5 returns globally — three weeks after the U.S. Department of Commerce placed the first-ever export directive on a frontier AI model, effectively taking it offline just days after launch.

Here's the backstory: On June 9, Anthropic released Fable 5, its most capable model yet — outperforming any previous Claude across software engineering, scientific reasoning, and complex knowledge work. But on June 12, federal regulators intervened after researchers discovered a technique that could bypass its safety guardrails.

What followed was unprecedented: an AI company working directly with the U.S. government to patch its model before re-release. The new safety classifier now blocks the specific jailbreak in over 99% of cases.

Starting today, Fable 5 is live for Claude.ai, Claude Code, and Claude Cowork users globally — with Pro, Max, and Team plans getting priority access through July 7.

This marks a new chapter: AI safety is no longer just a technical question. It's becoming a matter of national policy.
