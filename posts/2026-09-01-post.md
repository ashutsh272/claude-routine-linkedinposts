**Your AI coding tool just became a chess piece in a $60 billion power play.**

On August 28, OpenAI notified SpaceX that it will terminate model access for Cursor — one of the most popular AI coding assistants used by developers worldwide — with a cutoff date of November 12, 2026. The trigger: SpaceX's acquisition of Cursor for $60 billion.

OpenAI stated it "cannot be confident" that SpaceX will honor its usage terms, citing a history of disputes with Elon Musk's companies. The move immediately weaponized model supply as a competitive lever.

Anthropic didn't wait. Co-founder Tom Brown confirmed the company is ramping up Claude compute capacity for Cursor users — a move that underscores just how much enterprise ground Anthropic has already gained. Menlo Ventures data puts Anthropic at 40% enterprise AI spend share, ahead of OpenAI's 27%.

What this signals to every CTO and engineering leader: your AI model vendor is now a strategic dependency, not just a technology choice. Diversification, portability, and open-weight fallback options are no longer theoretical concerns.

This isn't just a contract dispute — it's a preview of how AI platform competition plays out at scale.
