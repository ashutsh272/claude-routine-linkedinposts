**One of the most watched AI companies is about to go public.**

Anthropic announced its IPO roadshow on November 10th. The company behind Claude is offering 825 million Class A shares on Nasdaq under the ticker $ANT, with an expected price range of $70–$78 per share.

What makes this listing unusual:
- Anthropic is offering 801M+ of those shares itself — meaning the bulk of proceeds go directly to the company
- At the top of the range, that's roughly $62B in gross proceeds to fund continued AI development
- It's a Public Benefit Corporation, legally bound to its mission of safe AI for humanity's benefit — not just shareholder returns

This IPO matters for the broader AI industry. It's the first major AI safety lab to go public, setting a valuation benchmark and signaling that safety-focused AI development is becoming a durable business model, not just a research project.

For anyone watching enterprise AI adoption, this is a milestone worth tracking closely.
