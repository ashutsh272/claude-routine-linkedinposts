**What if your AI transcription had a 5% error rate on phone calls — while the market average sits at 13–21%?**

xAI just made that claim real with Grok STT 1.0, a standalone speech-to-text API that went live on July 23. For teams running voice agents, call centers, or meeting transcription at scale, these numbers are hard to ignore.

On xAI's internal phone call entity recognition benchmark — names, account numbers, dates — Grok STT 1.0 scores a 5.0% word error rate versus:
- ElevenLabs: 12.0%
- Deepgram: 13.5%
- AssemblyAI: 21.3%

What the API ships with out of the box:
→ **25 languages** with real-time and batch modes
→ **Speaker diarization** and **word-level timestamps**
→ **12 audio formats** including narrowband telephony
→ **vad_threshold** tuning for noisy or quiet environments (useful for call center audio)
→ Pricing: **$0.10/hr batch | $0.20/hr streaming** — accessible via xAI API, OpenRouter, and Vercel AI Gateway

The broader signal: xAI is building a complete voice stack alongside Grok's language capabilities. Enterprise voice AI just got a credible new entrant with competitive pricing.
