Claude Sonnet 5.5 just rewrote the rules on what "mid-tier" AI means.

Anthropic released Claude Sonnet 5.5 on September 28th — and the benchmark jump is hard to ignore. On agentic coding (Terminal-Bench 4.0), Sonnet 5.5 scores 70.6%, up from just 10.3% on Sonnet 5. That's not an incremental update; that's a category shift.

What makes this significant for professionals: you now get near-Opus-level intelligence for everyday work — bug fixes, polished documents, spreadsheets, slides — at 30% faster speed and up to 30% lower cost per task. The model essentially matches Claude Opus 5.5 on knowledge-work benchmarks, at roughly half the operational cost.

For teams building AI-powered workflows, this changes the economics significantly. You no longer have to choose between performance and affordability — Sonnet 5.5 delivers both.
