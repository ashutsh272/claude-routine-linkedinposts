**Google shipped a model that completes three times more tasks than its predecessor — at the same price.**

Released September 2, Gemini 3.8 Flash is Google DeepMind's latest workhorse model, and the benchmarks are sharp. Compared to 3.7 Flash, it scores 73.7% on DeepSWE v1.1 (vs 65.3%), 89.4% on Terminal-Bench 2.1 (vs 85.8%), and 19.1% on the harder Terminal-Bench 4.0 (vs 11.2%). Across evaluations, it completes more than three times as many agentic tasks as its predecessor.

For teams running software agents, document-heavy workflows, or long multi-step reasoning pipelines, this is a meaningful capability jump — not a footnote update.

The pricing window is worth noting: $0.75 per million input tokens, $3.75 per million output tokens through December 31, 2026. Both figures double in January. The clock is ticking.

Google also introduced Gemini 3.8 Flash Cyber — a specialized variant for autonomous vulnerability discovery, available through the Fairwind Program to verified government authorities and critical infrastructure operators. Wiz reports +7.5–9.7% higher recall on penetration testing benchmarks at 2.3–5.2x lower cost vs other frontier models.
