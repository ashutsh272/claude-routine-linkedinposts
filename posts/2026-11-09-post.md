Anthropic just made the most anticipated AI IPO announcement of the decade.

On October 26, 2026, Anthropic, PBC filed its S-1 registration statement with the SEC — the first formal step toward listing on Nasdaq under the ticker "ANT." The company reported $16.3 billion in revenue in the first half of 2026 alone, a staggering jump from $1.2 billion in the same period a year earlier.

For professionals, this matters beyond the financial headlines. Anthropic going public means greater transparency, independent governance requirements, and a roadmap that must justify itself to public shareholders — not just strategic investors. The company's Public Benefit Corporation structure and AI safety mission will face a new level of accountability.

This is a turning point: not just for Anthropic, but for the entire AI industry. As foundational AI labs enter public markets, enterprise adoption, pricing, and policy decisions will all be shaped differently.
